How it works in detail →

See SignalLoop make a decision.

Pick a company, set the campaign goal and run one full cycle. Campaign results are simulated; recommendations and content are generated live by AI.

    Four decisions, one loop.

    Every recommendation answers the questions a product marketer asks after the campaign report.

    Who to target

    Weighs response against who actually buys, not just who clicks.

    Which channel

    Balances reach against the quality of response your goal needs.

    What message

    Tests your positioning one message at a time against what works today.

    What content

    Drafts the campaign for the approved test. You review it before it runs.
